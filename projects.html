Computer Vision • AI/ML Published Research

Advanced Face Recognition Attendance System (FRAS)

Researchers: Dr. Ayesha Heena & Mohsina Banu

An automated attendance management system leveraging Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for real-time facial feature extraction, matching, and logging. Designed to eliminate manual record keeping while ensuring high security and operational reliability.

Reported Performance: 98.5% recognition accuracy with an average inference latency of 0.5 seconds.

Biomedical AI • Biosignals Book Chapter Output

Analysis and Classification of Heart Abnormalities from ECG

Lead Researcher: Dr. Ayesha Heena

A biomedical signal processing framework for feature decomposition, noise filtration, and automated classification of cardiac arrhythmias from electrocardiogram (ECG) records. Published in Taylor & Francis CRC Press.

Domain: Healthcare Informatics & Clinical Decision Support

Trustworthy AI • Forensics Active Investigation

DeepFake Detection & Digital Facial Forensics

Research Focus: Mohsina Banu & Trustworthy AI Stream

Investigating spatial and temporal artifacts generated by generative adversarial networks (GANs) and diffusion models in synthetic facial imagery. Developing forensic metrics to detect manipulated media and prevent identity spoofing.

Focus: Artifact identification, frequency domain analysis & model robustness.

How Projects Progress

A rigorous engineering and academic standard applied to every club-affiliated project.

01

Literature & Problem Formulation

Teams conduct a deep literature survey to identify open gaps, benchmark state-of-the-art models, and specify quantifiable metrics for success.

02

Experimentation & Code Hygiene

Development is tracked through well-documented GitHub repositories with clear dependency definitions, unit tests, and reproducible scripts.

03

Output & Scholarly Documentation

Every mature project culminates in a verifiable output: a peer-reviewed publication, conference presentation, public software tool, or deployed prototype.
