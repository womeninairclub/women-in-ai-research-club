Research Areas

Where we explore AI.

Five primary research areas form the foundation of the club's research structure.

01

AI/ML Methodology

Exploring machine learning methods, statistical learning, model development, evaluation, experimentation and data-driven methodologies.

02

Biomedical AI

Investigating research opportunities where Artificial Intelligence can support biomedical and healthcare-related problems.

03

Explainable AI

Studying interpretability, explanation methods and ways to make AI systems easier to understand and evaluate.

04

Trustworthy AI

Exploring reliability, robustness, fairness, safety, transparency and responsible development of AI systems.

05

Research & Publications

Supporting the journey from research question and experimentation to technical documentation, manuscripts, preprints and publications.
