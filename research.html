Research & Innovation

Where we explore AI.

Our research and innovation streams provide a foundation for members to explore Artificial Intelligence, collaborate on research questions and develop meaningful ideas.

01
Stream 1

AI & Machine Learning

Exploring machine learning methodologies, model development, statistical learning, experimentation, evaluation and data-driven approaches to Artificial Intelligence.

Members can explore different methods, develop models, conduct experiments and investigate research questions across AI and machine learning.

02
Stream 2

Explainable & Trustworthy AI

Exploring how AI systems can become more interpretable, transparent, reliable, robust, fair and responsible.

This stream brings together research in Explainable AI, interpretability and trustworthy AI systems.

03
Stream 3

Biomedical AI

Exploring Artificial Intelligence applications involving biomedical data, medical imaging and signal analysis.

Members can investigate AI-based approaches to biomedical and healthcare-related research problems.

04
Stream 4

AI & IoT

Exploring the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Internet of Things technologies.

This stream focuses on intelligent connected systems, data-driven devices and applications combining AI with IoT.

05
Stream 5

Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Turning research ideas and emerging technologies into innovative projects, solutions and opportunities.

Members can explore problem-solving, project development, innovation and entrepreneurial thinking around AI.
