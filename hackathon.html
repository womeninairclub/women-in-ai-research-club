Participant Guide

Rules & Regulations

01
Eligibility

The hackathon is open to participants from different locations and backgrounds who complete the official registration requirements.

02
Team Size

Teams should consist of 2–4 members. Team information must be accurately represented in the registration and submission materials.

03
Originality

Submitted work must represent the team's own work. Plagiarism, copied solutions and misrepresentation of another person's work are not permitted.

04
Development Period

Teams must develop their solution during the official hackathon development period unless the organizers explicitly state otherwise.

05
External Resources

Libraries, frameworks, datasets and other external resources may be used where permitted, with appropriate attribution and license compliance.

06
AI-Assisted Tools

AI tools may be used where permitted. Teams remain responsible for their submitted work and must disclose significant AI assistance.

07
GitHub Repository

The submitted repository must be accessible to the judges and contain the required source code and documentation.

08
Deadline

All required submission materials must be submitted by 6:00 PM on 20 October 2026.

09
Prohibited Activity

Unauthorized access, attacks against systems, malicious code, disruption of other teams and other harmful or unlawful activity are prohibited.

10
Conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully with other participants, organizers, mentors and judges.

11
Submission Integrity

Teams must provide accurate project information, team details and links. Inaccessible or incomplete submissions may not be fully evaluated.

12
Organizer Authority

The organizers may clarify rules and address exceptional situations to protect the integrity and smooth operation of the event.

Final Submission

Submission Format

Your project submission should make it easy for the judges to understand, access and evaluate your work.

01
Build

Complete the selected challenge.

02
GitHub

Create an accessible project repository.

03
Document

Complete the required README and files.

04
Demo

Prepare a working demo or demonstration video.

05
Submit

Submit the repository and project details through the official form.

Required Project Information

Team name and team members
Selected challenge / problem statement
Project title
Abstract / project overview
Problem addressed and proposed solution
Methodology / implementation approach
Technologies and datasets used
GitHub repository URL
Demo or demonstration video URL
AI tools used and disclosure
Team contribution details

Recommended GitHub Structure

Keep the repository organized and make the README the first place judges can understand your project.

Projects are evaluated across the following areas.

Responsible Development

AI Tools Disclosure

Participants may use AI-assisted development tools where permitted by the challenge rules. Teams remain responsible for the accuracy, originality, security and functionality of their submitted work.

Significant use of AI tools should be disclosed in the project submission, including the tools used and the nature of their contribution where relevant.

Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Is registration free?

Yes. Registration and participation are free.

Is the hackathon online?

Yes. The AI Innovation Hackathon 2026 is an online event.

Who can participate?

The event is open to everyone who completes the official registration requirements.

How many people can be in a team?

Teams consist of 2–4 members.

When are the problem statements released?

The official problem statements are released at 10:30 AM on 20 October 2026.

Where do we submit the project?

The completed project is submitted through the official project-submission process, with the GitHub repository URL and required project information.

Is the registration form the project-submission form?

No. Registration and final project submission are separate steps.

Do I need GitHub?

Yes. The final submission requires an accessible GitHub repository containing the project materials.

Can we use AI tools?

AI-assisted tools may be used where permitted, but significant use should be disclosed and teams remain responsible for their work.
