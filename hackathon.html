Integrity & Guidelines

Rules & Regulations

All participants must adhere to the official Rules and Regulations. Compliance ensures fair judging, responsible engineering, and ethical AI development.

Team Formation & Conduct

Teams may consist of 1 to 4 participants. Each team must nominate a single Team Leader. Participants may belong to only one registered team. Professional, respectful communication is mandatory at all times.

Development & Code Originality

Core implementation must be produced during the hackathon development window. Pre-built entire applications are strictly disqualified. Open-source libraries, APIs, and public datasets are allowed with appropriate attribution.

GitHub Repository Requirements

Repositories must remain public until judging is complete. The repository must contain runnable code, dependency lists, reproduction instructions, and a README detailing solution architecture and results.

Responsible AI & Cybersecurity

Solutions must respect user privacy, model safety, and copyright. Do not commit sensitive API keys, credentials, or private patient data to public repositories.

Evaluation Rubric

Judging Criteria

Judges evaluate eligible final-round projects across six standardized dimensions.

01

Problem Understanding

Clarity in addressing the specific assigned challenge, problem depth, and domain relevance.

02

Innovation & Novelty

Originality of the proposed solution and creative utilization of modern computational approaches.

03

Technical Implementation

Code quality, robustness, architecture design, repository hygiene, and proper dependencies.

04

Meaningful AI Integration

Substantive and appropriate use of AI, machine learning, or deep learning methodologies.

05

Functionality & Real-World Impact

Working demonstration, practical feasibility, ethical considerations, and user experience.

06

Scalability & Presentation

Quality of documentation, future research roadmap, and clarity of the project pitch.
