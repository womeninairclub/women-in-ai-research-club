Academic & Club Leadership

01

Dr. Ayesha Heena

Founder & Faculty Coordinator

Dr. Ayesha Heena is an academician and researcher working across Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, image processing, wireless communication, sensor networks and healthcare-oriented applications.

She is dedicated to mentoring and guiding young women in engineering, Artificial Intelligence and research, encouraging them to explore emerging technologies and contribute meaningfully to the research community.

Her academic and research work includes contributions to AI and Machine Learning, biomedical applications, image processing, signal processing and related engineering domains.

As the Founder of the club, she provides the academic foundation and overall vision for building a community focused on research, innovation and entrepreneurship.

Research Interests:

  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Biomedical AI
  • Image Processing
  • Signal Processing
  • Wireless Communication
  • Sensor Networks

Role in the Club:
Founder and Faculty Coordinator, providing academic guidance, mentoring and direction for the club's research and innovation activities.

02

Prof. Dr. Inam Ullah Khan

Honorary Chief Academic & Research Advisor

Dr. Inam Ullah Khan is an academic and research professional whose work spans Artificial Intelligence, Artificial General Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Unmanned Aerial Vehicles, routing protocols and intrusion detection systems.

He is a Researcher at King's College London, UK and the Founder of AI-Explain You Science (AI-EYS), Lincoln University College Malaysia.

As the Honorary Chief Academic & Research Advisor of the club, he provides strategic academic guidance, advises on research directions and scientific activities, and supports members through research mentorship and expert interactions.

His advisory role also includes encouraging research ethics and quality, supporting collaborations and helping connect students with broader research opportunities and networks.

Research Areas:

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Artificial General Intelligence
  • Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning
  • Unmanned Aerial Vehicles
  • Routing Protocols
  • Intrusion Detection Systems
  • Evolutionary Computing
