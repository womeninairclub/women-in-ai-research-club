Founder & Faculty Coordinator

Dr. Ayesha Heena

B.E. (E&CE), M.Tech (Communication Systems), Ph.D. (VTU Belagavi)

Dr. Ayesha Heena is a Postdoctoral Researcher at AIEYS and a faculty member in the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at the Faculty of Engineering & Technology (Exclusively for Women), Sharnbasva University, Kalaburagi, Karnataka, India. She brings 25 years of distinguished teaching and research experience.

Her scientific findings have been published in prestigious SCI, Scopus, Google Scholar, and Web of Science-indexed journals. She currently serves as an active reviewer for reputed journals including IEEE Access, IETE, CSSE, CMC, and Springer Nature journals.

She authored the book chapter “Analysis and Classification of Heart Abnormalities” in the book “Biomedical Signal Processing for Healthcare Applications (Emerging Trends in Biomedical Technologies and Health Informatics)”, 1st Edition, published by Taylor & Francis CRC Press (London and New York).

Her honors include the International Academic and Research Excellence Award (Best Academician of the Year – Female, 2019), India Book of Records Achiever Award 2024 for 250+ certificates, Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Outstanding Educator Award (2025), and South Indian Women Achievers Award (2026). She holds multiple Indian and UK patent publications and grants, and has delivered 14 invited keynote lectures across several countries.

Honorary Chief Academic & Research Advisor

Prof. Dr. Inam Ullah Khan

Senior Member IEEE • Founder, AI-Explain You Science (AIEYS)

Prof. Dr. Inam Ullah Khan is a globally recognized academic, industry leader, and technological pioneer specializing in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), and Evolutionary Computing. His research spans Cryptography, UAVs, Routing Protocols, and Intrusion Detection Systems.

He holds a Postdoctoral Research Fellowship at Multimedia University (Malaysia), serves as a Professor at Lincoln University College, and supervises numerous PhD and Postdoctoral Fellows across the UK, Malaysia, India, and Pakistan. His international appointments include Senior Researcher at Azerbaijan University of Architecture and Construction (Baku), Global Mentor at IE University (Madrid), and Visiting Researcher at King's College London.

Dr. Khan collaborated with the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and the British Council to establish the National Curriculum for AI in Cybersecurity at the Master's and PhD levels, and authored five national curricula for FinTech and Banking AI.

His extensive research portfolio comprises over 150 authored research articles, editorship of 50+ books (with five volumes cataloged in the Yale University Library), and multiple international patents including UK design patents.

President

Mohsina Banu

B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Mohsina Banu serves as the President of the Women in AI, Research, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Club, steering community leadership, research collaboration, and innovation programs.

She specializes in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, and Digital Forensics. Her hands-on research and engineering experience encompasses automated facial recognition systems, emotion detection, sentiment analysis, and DeepFake detection, along with cybersecurity analysis and capture-the-flag competitions.

She is co-author of a Springer-published scientific book chapter on advanced face recognition technology (2025). As President, she actively coordinates with research team leads and faculty advisors to cultivate an inclusive, high-standard research environment.
