FOUNDER & FACULTY COORDINATOR

Dr. Ayesha Heena

Dr. Ayesha Heena acquired her B.E. (E&CE) from Gulbarga University, Gulbarga, and M.Tech (Communication Systems) from Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi, in 2001 and 2009 respectively. She completed her Ph.D. from Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi, in 2023.

She is a Post Doctoral Researcher at AIEYS and is a faculty member in the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at the Faculty of Engineering & Technology (Exclusively for Women), Sharnbasva University, Kalaburagi, Karnataka, India. She has 25 years of teaching experience.

Her research findings have been published in SCI, Scopus, Google-indexed and Web of Science journals. She currently serves as a reviewer for reputed journals including IEEE Access, IETE, CSSE, CMC and Springer Nature journals.

She has attended and presented research articles at numerous national and international conferences. She has also published a book chapter titled “Analysis and Classification of Heart Abnormalities” in the book “Biomedical Signal Processing for Healthcare Applications (Emerging Trends in Biomedical Technologies and Health Informatics)” , 1st Edition, published by Taylor & Francis CRC Press, London and New York.

Dr. Ayesha Heena has received several academic and professional recognitions, including the International Academic and Research Excellence Award (Best Academician of the Year – Female) in 2019, Best Paper Award, Best Presentation Award and the India Book of Records Achiever Award 2024 for a record of 250+ certificates from various programmes.

She has several patent publications and grants, including Indian and UK patents. She has also been recognized among the top 30 teachers in India according to AMP Voting. She has authored a book and received Gold and Silver Medals in various NPTEL courses.

She was awarded the Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Outstanding Educator Award at IEARG by MTTF on National Science Day, February 28, 2025. She has also been conferred the prestigious South Indian Women Achievers Awards 2026 in the category of Outstanding Educator Award.

A passionate teacher and learner, Dr. Ayesha Heena is also an international speaker who has delivered 14 invited plenary and keynote lectures across multiple countries at international events.

Her areas of interest include image processing, wireless communication, sensor networks, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

She is associated with WORK (World Organization for Religions and Knowledge), an NGO whose volunteers contribute to compassion activities across 300+ districts of India and several other countries. She is also a member of HWPL (Heavenly Culture, World Peace, Restoration of Light), a nonprofit international organization dedicated to promoting world peace and interfaith dialogue.

Her students' projects have been selected for KSCST Project Funding. She also serves as an Advisor for AIEYS Women in Computer Science and Innovation Club, Research and Innovation Mentor for Women in Engineering, and Judge for international events such as the Deadlock Mystery CTF competition.

She is actively involved in developing an AI Research Mentorship Framework for Women Engineering Students , reflecting her commitment to mentoring and empowering women in technology and research.

HONORARY CHIEF ACADEMIC & RESEARCH ADVISOR

Prof. Dr. Inam Ullah Khan

Dr. Inam Ullah Khan is a distinguished academic, industry leader, and technological pioneer specializing in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), and Evolutionary Computing.

His innovative work spans critical domains, including Cryptography, Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), Routing Protocols, and Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS). He is currently the Founder of AI-Explain You Science (AI-EYS).

Dr. Inam Ullah Khan holds a Postdoctoral Research Fellowship at Multimedia University, Malaysia, and serves as a Professor at Lincoln University College. He supervises many PhD and Postdoctoral Fellows across the UK, Malaysia, India and Pakistan.

His international roles include Senior Member of IEEE, Senior Researcher at the Azerbaijan University of Architecture and Construction in Baku, Azerbaijan, Global Mentor and Guest Lecturer at IE University, Madrid, and Visiting Researcher at King's College London.

At the national level, Dr. Inam Ullah Khan has contributed to educational policy and curriculum development. He collaborated with the HEC and the British Council to develop the National Curriculum for AI in Cybersecurity at the Master's and PhD levels.

He also authored five national curricula for FinTech, covering AI for Banking, Financial Institutions and data tools such as Power BI, which have been operational in Pakistan.

His prolific research portfolio includes more than 150 authored articles and the editorship of 50+ books. Notably, five of his volumes are officially cataloged in the Yale University Library.

An accomplished inventor, Dr. Inam Ullah Khan holds multiple patents, including a UK design patent for an agricultural drone and a solar-powered nutrient tracking device.

As a frequent technology expert on national and international television, Dr. Inam Ullah Khan remains a leading voice in emerging technologies and global technology policy.

PRESIDENT

Mohsina Banu

Mohsina Banu is the President of the Women in AI, Research, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Club Research Club, contributing to student leadership, research collaboration, community development and innovation initiatives.

She holds a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning and has a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Machine Learning, Computer Vision and Digital Forensics.
