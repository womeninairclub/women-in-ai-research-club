Member Experience

What You Can Do as a Member

A structured, supportive environment built for active growth and high achievement.

01

Join a Research Team

Collaborate with dedicated team leads across our five specialized research streams, contributing to empirical benchmarks and paper drafts.

02

Work on Real-World Projects

Gain hands-on experience building machine learning prototypes, engineering datasets, and evaluating models with industry-standard practices.

03

Access Faculty Mentorship

Receive strategic guidance on academic publishing, literature review methodology, and international research fellowships.

04

Participate in Hackathons

Compete in international online hackathons, solve challenging problem statements, and showcase your solutions to expert juries.

05

Co-Author Publications

Work toward turning experimental discoveries into peer-reviewed conference articles, book chapters, and indexed journal papers.

06

Grow as a Leader

Lead research tracks, organize symposiums, mentor junior students, and represent the club in global artificial intelligence forums.
