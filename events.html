Event Details

Two-Stage Hackathon

The hackathon follows a two-stage format. Only teams selected through the screening stage proceed to the Main Hackathon / Final Development Round.

01

Stage 1 — Idea Screening

Participants review the published problem statements and submit or pitch their proposed solution ideas.

  • Problem understanding
  • Proposed solution
  • Innovation / novelty
  • Proposed technology / AI approach
  • Expected impact
  • Brief implementation plan
02

Stage 2 — Main Hackathon

Selected teams develop and implement their proposed solutions during the designated development period.

  • Build and test the solution
  • Maintain a GitHub repository
  • Complete project documentation
  • Prepare demonstration / pitch
  • Submit through the official process

GitHub Repository Requirements

The final-round team repository must be accessible to the judges and should contain:

  • Source code
  • README.md
  • Requirements / dependencies
  • Installation instructions
  • Usage instructions
  • Documentation
  • Dataset information
  • Model information
  • Screenshots
  • Demo information
  • Credits / attributions

Evaluation

Judging Criteria

Problem Understanding

How clearly the team understands the assigned challenge.

Innovation

Originality and creativity of the solution.

Technical Implementation

Quality and appropriateness of the implementation.

Use of AI

Meaningful and appropriate use of AI/ML or related technologies.

Functionality

How effectively the proposed solution works.

Practicality & Impact

Potential real-world usefulness and impact.

Scalability

Potential for further development or deployment.

Presentation

Clarity of documentation, demonstration and final pitch.

The final judging criteria and weightage may be communicated separately by the organizers.
