Academic Programs

Our Event Series

Continuous learning and research programs organized throughout the academic calendar.

01

Technical Workshops

Hands-on coding masterclasses covering deep learning frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow), computer vision pipelines, biomedical signal toolkits, and reproducible research workflows.

02

Academic Seminars & Lectures

Distinguished guest lectures from international faculty, industry research leaders, and postdoctoral scholars presenting emerging breakthroughs in artificial intelligence.

03

Paper Reading Groups

Biweekly sessions where research stream members deconstruct seminal and recent papers from Top-tier AI venues (NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, IEEE TBME), analyzing methodology and limitations.

04

Competitions & CTFs

Competitive coding, machine learning challenges, capture-the-flag security events, and collaborative hackathons designed to translate conceptual theory into demonstrable prototypes.

05

Mentorship Roundtables

Small-group mentoring clinics where student researchers receive direct feedback on manuscript drafts, experimental design, and graduate research opportunities.
