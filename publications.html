Springer Publication · 2025

Enhancing Attendance Management: A Novel Approach Using Advanced Face Recognition Technology

Authors: Dr. Ayesha Heena & Mohsina Banu

This research presents a Face Recognition Attendance System (FRAS) designed to improve the accuracy, efficiency, security and real-time management of attendance using Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision technologies.

The work explores facial recognition, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), real-time processing and automated attendance management. The reported system achieved an overall recognition accuracy of 98.5%, with an average recognition time of 0.5 seconds.
