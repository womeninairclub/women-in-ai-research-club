Springer Publication • 2025 Peer-Reviewed Book Chapter

Enhancing Attendance Management: A Novel Approach Using Advanced Face Recognition Technology

Authors: Dr. Ayesha Heena & Mohsina Banu

This research presents an automated Face Recognition Attendance System (FRAS) engineered to improve recognition accuracy, operational throughput, and real-time biometric management using deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).

The paper explores image preprocessing, multi-angle feature embedding, and verification across diverse ambient lighting conditions. The proposed system achieved an overall recognition accuracy of 98.5% with an average recognition latency of 0.5 seconds.

Analysis and Classification of Heart Abnormalities

Author: Dr. Ayesha Heena

Published in the book “Biomedical Signal Processing for Healthcare Applications (Emerging Trends in Biomedical Technologies and Health Informatics)”, 1st Edition, CRC Press (London and New York).

Addresses clinical feature extraction and signal processing methodologies for automated electrocardiogram (ECG) interpretation and anomaly detection in cardiovascular diagnostics.
