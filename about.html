Who We Are

A research-first AI community.

Women in AI, Research, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Club is an independent academic and learning initiative focused on supporting women students in Artificial Intelligence, research, innovation and entrepreneurship.

About the Club

Learn. Research. Innovate. Lead.

The Women in AI, Research, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Club is being built as a collaborative academic community where women can explore Artificial Intelligence, develop research skills, work together on meaningful problems and transform ideas into innovation.

The club encourages members not only to learn and use AI, but also to contribute to its development, question its limitations and explore responsible and impactful ways of building AI systems.
