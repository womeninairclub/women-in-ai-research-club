Our Research Focus

Five Streams. One Research Community.

Our academic structure is anchored in five defined research areas, enabling members to specialize, form collaborative teams, and publish peer-reviewed scientific contributions.

01
Stream 1

AI/ML Methodology

Machine learning algorithms, deep neural architectures, predictive modeling, statistical learning, and rigorous experimental validation.

02
Stream 2

Biomedical AI / Image & Signal Analysis

Medical imaging analysis, physiological biosignals (ECG/EEG), clinical decision-support systems, and healthcare informatics.

03
Stream 3

Explainable AI (XAI)

Model interpretability, attribution methods, transparent decision boundaries, and understanding complex model representations.

04
Stream 4

Trustworthy AI

Safety, reliability, algorithmic fairness, uncertainty quantification, robustness against distribution shifts, and responsible AI.

05
Stream 5

Evaluation & Research Publications

Systematic benchmarking, reproducible scientific experiments, documentation, and advancing peer-reviewed journal and conference publications.
